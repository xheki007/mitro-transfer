Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 2026

Mitro Transfer (“we”, “our”, “us”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

  • Phone number (used for OTP authentication and account management)
  • Precise location data (used for ride booking, navigation, and real-time tracking)
  • Device or other IDs (used for app functionality and security)
  • Usage data (such as ride history and app interactions)

2. How We Use Your Data

  • Provide and operate taxi services
  • Authenticate users using OTP
  • Match passengers with drivers
  • Enable real-time ride tracking and navigation
  • Improve app performance and user experience
  • Ensure safety, prevent fraud, and comply with legal obligations

3. Location Usage

We use your precise location data to:

  • Detect pickup and drop-off locations
  • Provide navigation and route optimization
  • Enable real-time ride tracking

Location data is used only while the app is actively in use.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data.

We may share limited data with:

  • Drivers (for ride coordination)
  • Service providers (such as map and infrastructure providers)

5. Data Security

All data is transmitted securely using encryption in transit (HTTPS). We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal data only as long as necessary to provide our services and comply with legal obligations.
