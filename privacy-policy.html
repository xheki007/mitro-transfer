Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 2026

Mitro Transfer (“we”, “our”, “us”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

  • Phone number (used for OTP login)
  • Location data (used for ride booking, tracking, pickup and drop-off)
  • Usage data (such as ride history and app interactions)

2. How We Use Your Data

  • Provide taxi services
  • Match passengers with drivers
  • Track rides in real time
  • Improve app performance and user experience
  • Ensure safety and prevent fraud

3. Location Usage

We use your location data to:

  • Detect pickup and drop-off locations
  • Provide navigation and route optimization
  • Enable real-time ride tracking

Location is used only while the app is in use.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data.

We may share data with:

  • Drivers (for ride coordination)
  • Service providers (e.g., maps and analytics services)

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal data only as long as necessary to provide our services.
